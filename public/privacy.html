Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

sh-tboxd is a free, non-commercial map of NYU buildings where you can keep a diary of the places you visit and share it with friends. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account ID. Nothing else is requested from Google.
  • Your diary entries: the building, date, floor, rating, and notes you enter, plus when each entry was created and last edited.
  • Your profile: the username you choose and an optional bio you write.
  • Your friends: the friend requests you send and receive, and who you're friends with.
  • Your likes: which diary entries you've liked, and when.

The site uses no analytics, no ads, and no tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps you signed in.

How it's used

Only to sign you in, to show you your own diary, to show your profile to other users, and to share your diary with the friends you accept. Your information is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

Who can see your profile

Your username and bio are visible to anyone signed in to sh-tboxd, so other users can find you. Your name, email address, and Google profile picture are never shown to other users.

Who can see your diary

You and your friends. A friendship starts only when you send a request someone accepts, or accept one they send. Once you're friends, they can see all of your diary entries, including ones written before you became friends, how many you've written, and your friend list. Only you can add, change, or delete your entries. Unfriending someone, or declining their request, stops them seeing your diary right away. Other users who aren't your friends, and signed-out visitors, can't see your entries. The site's maintainer has administrator access to the database for upkeep and backups, and will not look at diary contents except to fix a problem you report.

Likes

You can like your own entries and your friends'. Anyone who can see an entry sees how many likes it has. Only the entry's author sees who liked it. You can unlike anything you've liked while you can still see it. If you're unfriended, your likes stay on their entries (still anonymous) until the entry or either account is deleted.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and handles sign-in, and the site is served by Vercel. Encrypted backups of the database may be kept for up to 90 days.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry at any time. To delete everything, sign in, open the account menu (your picture, top right), and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your profile, and all of your entries right away; copies in backups expire within 90 days. You can also revoke the site's access from your Google account settings.

Google user data

sh-tboxd's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The site is meant for university students and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions or requests can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
